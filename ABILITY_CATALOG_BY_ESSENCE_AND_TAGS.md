## Data schema

- `primary_essence`: primary existing favored essence (no new requirement)
- `primary_category`: one of Attack, Defense, Healing, Mobility, Summoning, Control or Utility
- `tags`: a flexible multi-tag list (includes the primary category and additional roles/mechanics)
- `favored_essences` and `compatible_aspects`: unchanged gameplay affinity/awakening metadata.

This classification is **mod-original design metadata**, not a claim about canonical novel abilities.
