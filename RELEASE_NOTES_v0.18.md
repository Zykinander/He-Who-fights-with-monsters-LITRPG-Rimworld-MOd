# Essencebound v0.18 — twenty advanced abilities

- Add 20 gameplay abilities for **70 playable powers total**, retaining the 100-design roster and marking 30 as future work.
- Introduce map cell and haulable item targeting as well as the existing pawn/self target interface.
- Add delayed impacts, world effect scheduling, temporary summoned allies, friendly buffs, hostile damage zones and restoration zones.
- Add short-range teleportation, a return-to-anchor effect, active damage reflection, adaptive combat buffs, enemy marks, and temporary obstructing barriers.
- Persist field duration, origin, caster, source essence/slot and summoned pawn references for save/load.
- Add eight new status effects (33 in total), one temporary building definition and eighteen new starter mappings for future essence absorptions.
- Preserve Iron through Diamond progress, per-essence awakening slots, affinity rolls, Gold/Diamond resistances and existing learned power IDs.

Compilation and runtime testing required. Power visuals and decoy/summon art remain placeholders.
